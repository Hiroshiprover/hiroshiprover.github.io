# Quantum Field Theory for Babies

## Preface

These are my personal notes from a quantum field theory course, namely "Introduction to Quantum Field Theory", taught by Sebastian Gracia Sanez at the Southern University of Science and Technology. According to Seb, the course does not follow any specific textbook, only a few recommended readings. As far as I can tell, though, it mainly follows Schwartz's book: apart from removing some introductory material and using the opposite sign convention for the metric, the logical order is the same. Here is a [link](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2044797724638413585) to a Chinese translation.

The first draft of these notes was in fact extracted directly from Seb's blackboard writing by ChatGPT and DeepSeek; on that basis I polished (rewrote) them by hand and filled in some of my own understanding and examples. The notes currently reach the real scalar field.

I strongly condemn the tendency of AI companies to care only about coding ability and to neglect natural-language writing when they train models. I have to spend an extraordinary amount of time working out what the correct yet utterly unreadable nonsense written by the AI actually means, which adds roughly $11.4514\%$ to the difficulty of organizing these notes ().
